## Macro Summary

# Macro Thesis: Credit Risk in the AI Trade and Geopolitical Stress

**1. What's driving markets now**
The latest readings (Sept 1, 2026) put the fed funds rate at **3.75%** and unemployment at **4.2%**. Money isn't cheap, but the Fed isn't squeezing hard either, and the job market looks steady. CPI came through only as an index level (336.3) with no earlier reading, so I can't say whether inflation is speeding up or slowing down. The main story in the headlines is **credit stress threatening the AI bull market**, with "all eyes on Oracle." Questions about the cost and local impact of data centers (Google) add to that pressure. Geopolitical risk is also high: Houthi attacks pushed Saudi Arabia to postpone its investor conference to 2027, and France faces violent protests on top of debt-crisis fears, which have hit French stocks, French bonds and the euro.

**2. What's likely to matter in the coming days to weeks**
- Oracle's credit health as a test for AI spending financed by debt
- Bond market fragility, with one headline warning that hedge funds could make a "bond market shock more likely"
- Whether France's debt problems spread to other European markets
- A possible Putin–Trump–Xi summit on Ukraine, held without Zelenskyy

**3. What this could mean for each asset**
- **Stocks:** AI-heavy names carry the most risk if Oracle stumbles. Defense looks supported, with JPMorgan putting its first $5B of defense investments to work.
- **Gold:** Middle East escalation, unrest in Europe and bond-market worries all tend to favor gold as a hedge.
- **Crypto:** None of the headlines mention crypto directly. If credit stress drags risk assets down, crypto would probably fall too, but that's an inference, not something the data shows.

**Caveat:** The tools gave me no price, yield or inflation-trend data. Check those before making any trades.
